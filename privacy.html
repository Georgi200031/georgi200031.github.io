HIGHWAY: ROADSTER

Privacy Policy

Highway: Roadster · Android package com.highwayroadster
Effective date: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Highway: Roadster (also called Roadster Sky Run) handles information. The developer is Georgi Valeriev Rusev. Contact: gogo.rusev31@gmail.com.

1. Game progress on your device

The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. Coins, best distance and vehicle upgrade levels are saved locally in the app's storage. The developer does not operate a game server that receives this saved progress. The game has no chat or public player profiles.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show advertisements, including full-screen ads after a completed run and optional rewarded ads. The SDK automatically collects and shares information with Google for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This can include:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, including Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hangs and energy usage.

The developer does not receive a copy of your Google account password or direct access to your Google account. Google's handling is explained in its Privacy Policy, partner services notice and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Ad availability and personalisation depend on Google, your region, settings and any applicable consent.

3. Advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent where Google requires it. Where privacy options are available, use AD PRIVACY in the app to revisit them. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android's advertising privacy settings. Watching rewarded ads is optional. Choosing not to watch them does not prevent you from playing.

4. Support messages

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the contents you choose to send are used to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is not used to build an advertising profile.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage permanently removes coins, records and upgrades saved by this app. There is no developer-managed player account to delete. To request deletion of support correspondence held by the developer, email the contact above. It is retained only as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant records or meet applicable obligations. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies; the developer cannot directly delete Google's advertising records. Use Google's privacy controls for those records.

6. Security and transfers

The SDK uses TLS encryption for data sent to Google. Game progress is stored in the app's local storage. No system can guarantee absolute security. Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than yours under their published privacy and transfer safeguards.

7. Children and families

The game does not ask children to submit names or contact details. Parents or guardians with concerns about a child's information can contact the developer. Check the Google Play listing for the game's age rating and suitability. Advertising and device privacy settings can be managed using the controls described above.

8. This website

This support website does not use advertising, analytics tags, sign-in forms or its own tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. External links are governed by their providers' policies.

9. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact Georgi Valeriev Rusev at gogo.rusev31@gmail.com.
